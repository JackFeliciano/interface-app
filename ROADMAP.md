## Roadmap

The project currently holds one sentence engine that labels words by part of speech, derives other forms, works with five fixed sentence structures, draws vocabulary from a frequency list and practises through a conversation step. Its hand-written test runner contains 41 checks.

1. Expand the vocabulary coverage while keeping the frequency list as the source for word selection.
2. Extend the word analysis so learners can explore the relationship between a word and its derived forms.
3. Develop the five sentence structures with more practice material for each structure.
4. Improve the conversation step so practice can draw on sentence analysis and the vocabulary already introduced.
5. Extend the test runner to cover new vocabulary, derived forms, sentence structures and conversation behaviour.
